Chronax ↗

  • Statistical and neural forecasting models under a unified, scikit-like API
  • Built on JAX: compiled and vectorized, so the same code runs on GPUs and TPUs for fast fitting across many series at once
  • Compare the precision of each model's forecasts with native conformal prediction

TempusBench ↗

  • Benchmarks foundation models against classical ones on 48 forecasting tasks
  • New datasets that foundation models weren't pretrained on
  • Every model tuned under the same protocol, so comparisons are fair

About Us

Totally Tabular is an open-source community for anyone interested in machine learning for tabular data. We believe that community plays an important role in the advancement of research by uniting many different perspectives under a common goal. At Totally Tabular, experts will find peers ready to discuss the latest research developments, and novices can expect high-quality resources and discussions to further their own learning. Whether you’re a researcher, an industry practitioner, or just trying to finish a paper instead of bookmarking it forever, Totally Tabular has a place for you.

As an open-source community, we’re looking to make real contributions to the field. To start, we’re organizing a discussion-based reading group that reviews research papers. These discussions not only enhance our own understanding of the literature, but allow us as a community to pursue the ideas we find interesting. Our projects, articles, and research are community-driven; we want to address the problems you are interested in. Join us on Discord, or sign up for the Total Tabloid, and let’s get reading.
